# Ikh Zasag Rulebook — Competition Addendum v1

## Fairness: No Manual Match Control Rule

Nuudeltsgeey prohibits manual opponent assignment. Organizers may configure tournament style, but the system exclusively generates brackets, seeds, and progressions automatically under Ikh Zasag logic to ensure fairness, transparency, and anti-corruption.

No administrator picks matches, no host fixes brackets, and no player targets an individual opponent. Control exists at the format level, not the person level.

## Arena Day Rule

Nuudeltsgeey Major Competitions are held only on designated Arena Days. These days carry cultural, seasonal, or historical meaning. Arena Days are limited to preserve honor, pressure, and national identity.

Daily Arena competitions continue at cafés, schools, floors, and clans. National Arena Days are rare major competitions with invitation and qualification requirements. Designating a date does not permit manual opponent control or override the participation-based significance engine.

See the [Tournament Operations Manual](./tournament-operations.md) for configuration and calendar behavior.

## Optional Bökh mode scope clarification

Its Zaan+ challenge rights are confined to consenting mode participants within the permitted rank range. They do not authorize hosts or admins to assign regular tournament opponents. Daily Bökh matching and ceremonial bracket seeding remain automatic. Existing standard competition rules remain unchanged.

## Confirmed tournament operations clarifications

Only Floor 2 receives lateness protection; this is not protection from cheating or other violations. Hosts may choose style but cannot influence the system-generated randomized draw, seeds, or progression. Randomization must respect the selected style’s constraints. Only admins may create Grand Battles; the existing Arena Day restriction remains. Admins still cannot assign opponents or manually determine event importance.

## Beginner style access

Floors 1–2 use Open Bracket only, keeping competition simple for beginners. Additional styles unlock as players climb. Eligibility uses the lowest floor among all participants, including teammates. Any Floor 1–2 participant restricts the event to Open Bracket. If everyone is Floor 3+, the host chooses from styles unlocked for the entire group. The host’s personal floor is irrelevant unless the host also plays. Only the exact higher-floor unlock schedule remains undefined. The separate optional Bökh mode keeps its own cultural rank and rules.

Brackets generate only after the roster and tournament settings are locked. This is a confirmed requirement, not an implemented bracket service.

## Current disciplinary process — approved update

This section supersedes the earlier automatic permanent-removal policy, including economic and optional-mode references.

Report → preserve evidence → administrator review → player/team response → decision → penalty if required. Record status as Reported, Under Review, or Violation Established. An accusation alone is not proof; insufficient evidence must not become a confirmed-cheating record.

| Level | Available consequence |
| --- | --- |
| I | Warning for minor violations |
| II | Published competitive penalty, including a justified forfeit |
| III | Removal from the current tournament |
| IV | Suspension with a specified duration or conditions |
| V | Badge revocation for the most serious or repeated violations |

Penalty selection considers seriousness, evidence, advantage gained, impact, intent, and repeated behavior. These are levels of severity, not a requirement to issue every lower level first. No automatic token confiscation, burning, or wallet disabling is introduced. Public disciplinary reporting remains deferred.

Only Floor 2 receives lateness protection. Floor 1 does not. This does not change the separate existing early-floor match-result rank protection or the Open Bracket-only rule for Floors 1–2. Whether lateness protection prevents a team forfeit remains undefined; do not implement that exception by assumption.

## Identity, conduct, and administrator accountability

One player uses one registered competitive identity; documented account correction or replacement is an administrative exception. Prohibit account sharing, identity substitution, spectator assistance, unfair software/scripts, prohibited exploits, match fixing, false results, and knowingly falsified evidence.

Normal competitive celebration is allowed; threats, discriminatory harassment, targeted abuse, physical confrontation, equipment damage, and deliberate disruption are prohibited. Immediate venue safety action is separate from establishing a cheating violation.

Administrators must apply published rules consistently, preserve evidence, record significant decisions, protect player information, and avoid favoritism. A significant conflict of interest should be referred to another authorized administrator where possible. Operational discretion never permits selecting opponents or inventing rules to favor a team.

Each tournament identifies its rule version before play. Material rules remain fixed once play starts except necessary documented technical, safety, integrity, or operational interventions communicated to affected teams. Such intervention does not authorize an opponent reroll.

## Roster clarification

For 5v5, lock five starters and one optional registered substitute before bracket generation. Post-lock changes are substitutions from that locked roster, not new entrants. Opponents and bracket positions never change. Major Arena tournament substitutions require administrator approval. Ordinary café approval authority and substitution timing remain pending.
