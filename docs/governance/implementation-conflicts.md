## Result and settlement boundary proposal

Store submission revisions and active lineups. Confirmations bind to an exact revision. Agreement triggers substantiated validation; disputes go to independent review. Record official-result notification separately from final settlement. Advance only eligible winners without affected-result holds. Keep settlement pending until applicable filing requirements and result-affecting cases/appeals are satisfied. Apply the unchanged NR formula through an idempotent transaction; corrections append linked reversals/replacements rather than erasing history. Apply equivalent holds to tournament awards. Snapshot/order semantics remain GOV-031, not an implicit policy choice.
