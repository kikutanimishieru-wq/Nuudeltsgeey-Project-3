Version: 1.0  
System Name: Nuudeltsgeey Movement  
Platform: eNomad Arena  
Core Law: Ikh Zasag  
Purpose: Build a fair, reputation-driven digital economy for competition, identity, and national development.

> Product proposal supplied by the project owner. Paragraph formatting has been normalized. This is not a description of implemented services.

## 1. Executive Summary

The eNomad Arena Token Economy is designed to reward participation, discipline, consistency, skill, and community contribution.

Unlike gambling or pay-to-win systems, the economy follows Ikh Zasag principles: Honor, Streak, Contribution, and Fair Play create value. Money only accelerates access, never replaces merit.

Tokens are not only for gaming. They are a digital reputation-backed medium of exchange inside cafés, schools, tournaments, and later real-world services. This system turns players into economic actors, not just users.

---

## 2. Philosophy of the Token

Traditional systems reward only winners. Nuudeltsgeey rewards showing up, staying disciplined, improving, competing fairly, and helping the ecosystem.

Core beliefs: Everyone can earn. Winners earn more. Cheaters earn nothing. Participation builds national power.

Tokens represent trust, activity, skill, and reputation. Not speculation.

---

## 3. Token Types

Arena Token (AT) is the primary currency of eNomad Arena. Used for tournament entry, café products, event hosting, badge upgrades, league access, and casting rewards. Earned by playing, winning, streaks, hosting, casting, and streaming.

Badge Token (BT), a later-stage token, supports cosmetic badges, clan visuals, profile customization, and trophy upgrades. This keeps the economy healthy without pay-to-win.

---

## 4. Token Distribution (Mint Logic)

Tokens are minted through activity, not speculation.

Participating → Base reward; Winning → Bonus reward; Win streak → Multiplier; Hosting → Event reward; Casting → Media reward; Streaming → View-based reward; Clan contribution → Shared pool.

Token Earned = Base × Event Weight × Rank Factor × Streak Multiplier

Base = participation value. Event Weight = size and rank density. Rank Factor = opponent strength. Streak Multiplier = discipline bonus.

---

## 5. Token Sink (Burn Logic)

To avoid inflation, tokens are spent (burned) through tournament entry, floor access, café purchases, clan creation, badge upgrades, league registration, and event hosting.

Every cycle: Earn → Use → Circulate → Burn → Rebuild. No hoarding advantage.

---

## 6. Streak Economy

Discipline creates value. Players earn streaks by daily participation, weekly competition, and consistent play.

Streak affects token multiplier, rank multiplier, and floor access. If a streak breaks, rewards decrease, access reduces, and growth slows.

Ikh Zasag principle: Honor grows daily, not instantly.

---

## 7. Pay + Earn Hybrid Model

Players can earn tokens, buy tokens, receive sponsor tokens, and receive café tokens.

Buying only unlocks entry. Winning unlocks growth. Discipline unlocks prestige. No pay-to-win dominance.

---

## 8. Betting Logic (Controlled Stage)

Later stage: Players may stake Arena Tokens. Only system tokens allowed. No external cash gambling. Law controlled.

If you lose: Tokens reduce and rank is affected. If you win: Tokens multiply and reputation rises.

Whitepaper position: “Betting is competitive staking, not gambling.” This is the proposal’s terminology, not a legal determination. Staking is not implemented in this prototype.

---

## 9. Token Wallet System

Each user has a wallet address, balance, history, earned, and spent totals.

Supports QR payments, café POS, membership sync, and sponsor transfers. Security: Device binding, QR validation, and anti-fraud logic.

---

## 10. Café Integration Economy

Cafés become economic nodes. Cafés can issue starter tokens, reward attendance, accept tokens for food and playtime, host tournaments, and sponsor players.

This keeps money inside Mongolia instead of leaking abroad.

---

## 11. Clan Economy

Clan token pools include shared earnings, tournament rewards, and sponsor rewards.

Clan leaders distribute player rewards, travel support, and entry support. Clans become micro-economies.

---

## 12. Anti-Cheat Economic Law

Updated policy: confirmed violations receive graduated penalties after evidence review and player response. Badge revocation is the highest level, not an automatic outcome. This supersedes the original automatic badge-removal, token-freeze, rank-erasure, and wallet-disable rule. See the [current rulebook](./ikh-zasag-rulebook.md).

Reputation > Money.

---

## 13. Data as Economic Asset

The platform collects performance data, participation data, and regional data.

Later: Scouts subscribe, organizations access analytics, and universities analyze talent. Revenue comes from data intelligence, not player exploitation.

---

## 14. Inflation Control

To protect value: Daily mint limits, floor access costs, seasonal resets, trophy rarity tiers, and burn events.

Healthy economy > fast growth.

---

## 15. National Economic Impact

Nuudeltsgeey Token Economy supports youth employment, digital identity, café business growth, tourism via events, education via discipline systems, and international esports exports.

It builds Mongolia’s human capital economy, not only mineral economy.

---

## 16. Roadmap for Token Economy

Phase 1 — Closed Economy: Local tokens, café usage, and tournament entry.

Phase 2 — National Economy: Clan pools, sponsorship, and scout tools.

Phase 3 — International Economy: Cross-border events, multi-currency bridge, and identity export.

---

## 17. Risk Management

Risks: Inflation, cheating, pay-to-win abuse, and token hoarding.

Solutions: Burn mechanics, rank protection, streak decay, and anti-cheat law.

---

## 18. Closing Statement

The eNomad Arena Token is not crypto speculation. It is competitive reputation energy.

Players do not mine with machines. They mine with discipline, honor, and participation.

Nuudeltsgeey turns gaming into education, economy, identity, and national power.
